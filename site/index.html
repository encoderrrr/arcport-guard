01 / WHY

EVM-compatible does not mean identical.

Arc keeps the Ethereum developer experience while changing the assumptions that matter most for programmable money.

186
PRECISION

One USDC balance. Two decimal interfaces.

Native USDC uses 18 decimals while the ERC-20 interface uses 6. ArcPort finds raw comparisons before they become expensive accounting bugs.

0x00
RANDOMNESS

Predictable is not random.

PREVRANDAO returns zero on Arc. We flag insecure randomness at the source.

≡
ORDERING

Same timestamp. Different blocks.

Sub-second blocks can share a timestamp. Use block numbers when order matters.

PendingFinal
FINALITY

No confirmation theater.

Arc finalizes on inclusion. ArcPort catches unnecessary multi-confirmation waits that make fast settlement feel slow.
