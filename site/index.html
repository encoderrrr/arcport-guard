03 / WHY ARCPORT

EVM-compatible does not mean behaviorally identical.

Arc changes several assumptions that Ethereum-focused tooling may not surface during a normal local test.

18 ↔ 6

USDC precision

Native and ERC-20 interfaces share a balance but expose different decimal precision.

0x00

Randomness

PREVRANDAO returns zero, making Ethereum-style randomness predictable.

≡

Block ordering

Sub-second blocks can share timestamps, so ordering needs block-aware keys.

1 conf

Finality

Transactions are final on inclusion; extra confirmation waits add avoidable latency.
